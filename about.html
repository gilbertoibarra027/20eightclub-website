About 20eightClub

South Florida's supercar meet.

Gear 1 · Our story

Not the same car club.

20eightClub started in Weston in 2024 with one idea: bring South Florida's best supercars, and the people behind them, into one place. Every quarter we take over Weston Town Center for a morning that's part car show, part block party.

Weston is where it started, but it's not where it stops. Future meetups are heading to more spots across Broward.

Gear 2 · What we do

What we do.

01

Meets & drives
